The menu bar app is easier to use with the keyboard and easier to read. ↑ and ↓ move through the window's sidebar again, and rows move with ⌥⌘↑ and ⌥⌘↓ or **Move up** and **Move down** in their context menu; while you drag one, the marker shows where it will land. Grey captions are darker in light mode, so they meet WCAG AA contrast. Link, Restore and the window's other buttons show a spinner while their job runs, and the sidebar shows what dryfit is doing. The menu can restore an app after a failed install, and the right-click menu can start dryfit as well as stop it. **New group** won't overwrite a group with the same name. Settings now shows its errors instead of dropping them.
