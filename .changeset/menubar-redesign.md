The menu bar app gets a redesigned menu: each library is a card you can fold, with one-click links for the apps that use it, sync progress on the card, and Restore under the pointer. Settings moves into the dryfit window (the gear in the sidebar, or ⌘,), and the old ⋯ menu moves to a right-click on the menu bar icon. Drag libraries, apps and groups in the sidebar to order them; the menu follows. Installing dryfit from the app now says why it failed instead of offering the button again, and an app added by hand no longer shows up twice once linked.
